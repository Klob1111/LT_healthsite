Nutrition.

Food. Pretty important. It's proven to have a pretty big connection to mental health, especially along with exercise. Inflammation, one of the key symptoms of poor diet, is shown to have a negative link to the areas of the brain associated with reward pathways, and a positive connection with the areas linked to anxiety. The subject is under-researched, but most evidence points to a connection between nutrition and mental health. There's a lot to nutrition, so I won't go over all of it. Overall the connection between mental health and eating is weaker than sleep and exercise, but is still quite significant. Many of the studies may just be reflecting a boost in self confidence and security, coming from a diet one deems "healthy". Poor access to healthy food could also prove problematic for the connection. Regardless, it's clear that there's something there, and eating well is obviously great for your physical health, so keep paying attention!
